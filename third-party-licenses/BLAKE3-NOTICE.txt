Static builds download and link the verified official BLAKE3 1.8.5 C sources
with runtime SIMD dispatch. Non-static development builds use the portable
fallback in internal/nativev4/blake3_portable.c and
internal/nativev4/blake3_portable.h. Both implementations are distributed
under the Apache License, Version 2.0; see BLAKE3-APACHE-2.0.txt.
